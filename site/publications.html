Working Notes

A Proposed Framework for Advanced (Multi)Linear Infrastructure in Engineering and Science (FAMLIES)

FAMLIES Working Note #0, arXiv:2604.07311, 2026

Authors: D.A. Matthews, T.M. Low, M.E. Myers, D.N. Parikh, R.A. van de Geijn

This paper introduces FAMLIES, a comprehensive framework for vertically integrating dense linear and tensor algebra software. We present the vision, research objectives, and architectural principles that enable cross-layer fusion and communication-avoiding algorithms across the full computation stack, from micro-kernels to distributed GPU clusters.
